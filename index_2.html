About Me

Getting to know me

I'm Naveena K, an enthusiastic Electronics and Communication Engineering student with a growing focus on the semiconductor and VLSI industry. I enjoy working at the intersection of hardware and logic — taking a design concept from RTL to a verified, working simulation.

My core interests lie in VLSI design, RTL design, Verilog HDL, digital electronics, FPGA design, and digital system design. I'm building my knowledge of semiconductor technology through structured coursework combined with independent, hands-on projects.

I approach every project methodically — understanding the specification, designing the logic, writing clean RTL, and verifying it thoroughly through simulation before calling it done.
